Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open CoinPing, navigate to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do price alerts work?

Set an alert for a coin at a price, above or below. Our server checks prices every minute. An alert fires when the price stays beyond your level for two consecutive checks, so a brief dip does not trigger a false alarm. Each alert fires once; you can re-arm it in the app, or it re-arms itself once the price moves back past your level.

Do I need to manually refresh prices?

No. CoinPing updates prices every 30 seconds and refreshes charts automatically while the app is open. There is no need to pull to refresh or leave and come back.

Where is my data stored?

Your coin list and alerts are stored on your device and synced across your devices via your personal iCloud account. Your alert definitions and device token are also held by our Cloudflare Worker so it can send you push notifications. We do not maintain external database servers to store your history. Price data comes from Coinbase and CoinGecko.

How do I change the currency?

Open Settings and pick a currency. Prices are shown in your device's local currency by default.

Why didn't I get an alert?

Check that notifications are turned on for CoinPing in your iPhone Settings, that the alert is still active (each alert fires once, then you re-arm it), and that you have an internet connection. Alerts are sent as time sensitive notifications, so they break through Focus and sleep mode.

Are there limits on coins and alerts?

The free tier includes 3 coins and 1 price alert. Pro, a one time $4.99 purchase, includes unlimited coins, unlimited alerts, and percent change alerts.
