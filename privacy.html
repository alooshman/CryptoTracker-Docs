Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

CoinPing ("the App"), developed by Ali Nazzal ("we", "our", or "us"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the CoinPing mobile application.

1. Data Storage & Syncing

CoinPing is architected with privacy-first principles:

  • Local & Personal Cloud Storage: Your coin list and price alert definitions are stored locally on your device and synchronized across your authorized devices using your personal operating system cloud backup account. Your data resides solely in your private personal storage container. We do not operate external database servers storing your coin list or alert history, nor do we have access to your stored records.
  • Price Alerts & Push Notifications: When you set a price alert, your alert definition, a per-install device identifier, and device push token are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to our Cloudflare Worker solely so it can evaluate your alert and send you a push notification when it triggers. Alert data is stored only for as long as the alert is active; deleting an alert in the app removes it from the Worker. Your device token is never used for any other purpose.
  • Price Data: The app fetches public cryptocurrency price data from Coinbase and CoinGecko. Those services may see your IP address when the app requests price data, as any web request does. We do not send them any personal information.

2. In-App Purchases & Billing

All purchase transactions are processed securely through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system and verified via industry-standard purchase verification infrastructure. We do not collect, view, or store your payment card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We only work with trusted third-party infrastructure and service sub-processors strictly necessary to deliver core application functionality:

  • Personal Cloud Backup Services: To enable secure cross-device synchronization tied to your personal device account.
  • Cloudflare Workers: To evaluate your price alerts and deliver push notifications.
  • Price Data Providers (Coinbase and CoinGecko): To provide live cryptocurrency price data.
  • App Store & Purchase Verification Services: To process digital transactions, validate receipts, and manage purchase entitlements.

4. Analytics & Tracking

CoinPing does not track you across third-party applications or websites, nor do we sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your coin list and alert definitions are retained exclusively on your local device and within your private personal cloud storage container. Deleting the CoinPing application from a device removes the local database copy on that device. Synced records remain in your private cloud storage until you delete them directly within the app or manage them via your device's system cloud storage settings. Deleting an alert in the app also removes it from the Cloudflare Worker.

6. User Responsibility for Data Backups

CoinPing operates on a local-first and personal cloud architecture. We do not maintain server-side backups of your coin list or alerts. You are solely responsible for maintaining adequate backups of your device and cloud storage. We are not liable for any lost, corrupted, or unrecoverable data resulting from device loss, app deletion, hardware failure, or cloud sync errors.

7. Accuracy of Price Data & Alerts

Cryptocurrency prices are provided for informational purposes only and may be delayed, inaccurate, or temporarily unavailable. Price alerts are delivered on a best effort basis; we do not guarantee that an alert will be delivered or delivered at a specific time. You acknowledge that prices and alerts do not constitute financial or investment advice, and you are solely responsible for your own investment decisions.

8. Children's Privacy

CoinPing does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Accessibility Statement

We are committed to digital accessibility and ensuring our software and web content conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using CoinPing or our web services, please email us at snicks.legate-7y@icloud.com and we will actively work to resolve the issue.
